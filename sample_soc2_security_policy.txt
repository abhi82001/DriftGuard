Acme Cloud Inc. - Information Security Policy (v3.2, effective 2026-01-15)
Owner: CISO. Approved by: CEO. Review cycle: annual.

1. Access Control
1.1 All production access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA).
1.2 User access reviews are required quarterly. The last review was completed on 2026-07-02 by the Security team.
1.3 Terminated employees have access revoked within 24 hours of termination.
1.4 Administrative privileges are granted on a least-privilege basis and approved by a manager.

2. Change Management
2.1 All production changes require peer review and approval before deployment.
2.2 Emergency changes may be deployed without prior approval and are reviewed within 5 business days.

3. Logging and Monitoring
3.1 Security events are collected centrally and retained for 90 days.
3.2 Alerts for critical events are reviewed by the on-call engineer.

4. Encryption
4.1 Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
4.2 Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.

5. Backup and Recovery
5.1 Backups are performed daily and are encrypted.
5.2 Backups are retained for 30 days.
5.3 Disaster recovery restore tests are performed annually. The last restore test was not completed in 2025.
5.4 Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 4 hours. Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is 24 hours.

6. Incident Response
6.1 An incident response plan exists and is reviewed annually.
6.2 Incident response tabletop exercises are not currently performed.

7. Vendor Management
7.1 Critical vendors are assessed before onboarding.
7.2 Vendor SOC 2 reports are not collected on a recurring basis.

8. Contradictory statements (for conflict testing)
8.1 Access reviews are performed quarterly.
8.2 Access reviews are performed annually.
8.3 MFA is required for all users.
8.4 MFA is optional for internal administrators.

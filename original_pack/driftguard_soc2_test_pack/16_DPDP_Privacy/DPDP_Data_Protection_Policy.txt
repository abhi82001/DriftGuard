ACME CLOUD INC. - DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (DPDP) ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE POLICY
Version 1.4, effective 2026-02-01. Owner: Data Protection Officer. Approved by: CEO. Review cycle: annual.

1. Consent and Notice
1.1 Personal data of Indian data principals is processed only after free, specific, informed consent is obtained and recorded.
1.2 A notice describing the personal data collected and the purpose is provided before or at the time of consent.
1.3 Data principals may withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal is processed within 7 days.

2. Data Principal Rights
2.1 Requests for access, correction and erasure are acknowledged within 7 days and resolved within 30 days.
2.2 A grievance redressal officer is published and grievances are resolved within 30 days.

3. Personal Data Breach
3.1 Personal data breaches are reported to the Data Protection Board of India and affected data principals without delay.
3.2 Breach notification to the Board is completed within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
3.3 Breach notification to the Board is completed within 7 days of becoming aware of the breach.

4. Retention and Erasure
4.1 Personal data is erased when the purpose is fulfilled or consent is withdrawn.
4.2 Personal data is retained for 3 years after the last interaction, then erased.
4.3 Personal data is retained for 7 years after the last interaction.
4.4 Erasure of cloud-stored personal data is performed by cryptographic erasure (KMS key destruction).

5. Children's Data
5.1 Verifiable parental consent is required before processing the personal data of a child.

6. Cross-Border Transfers and Processors
6.1 Personal data is transferred only to countries not restricted by the Central Government.
6.2 Data processors are engaged under a written contract containing DPDP safeguards.
6.3 Annual DPDP assurance reviews of processors are not currently performed.

7. Security Safeguards
7.1 Personal data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher.
7.2 Access to personal data requires multi-factor authentication and is reviewed quarterly.
